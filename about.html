About Trideca

Trideca was built by DBT students for the small dukas that keep our estates running. Many shop owners still count stock by memory or in an exercise book, and that leads to lost sales and expired goods.

Our goal

To give every small shop a simple, affordable way to know what it has, what it sold, and what to restock — from a phone.

How it works

First, the owner adds products. Then, every sale is recorded. Trideca adds up the totals and shows which items are running low.
